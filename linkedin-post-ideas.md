# LinkedIn post ideas from "A Working App Is Not a Platform"

Source: content/posts/vibe-coded-apps-are-not-platforms.md (draft, not yet published)

Eight standalone posts plus a teaser for the article. Space them out over
several weeks so it reads as a series rather than recycling. Only two or
three should link back to the article; the rest stand on their own.

| # | Source section | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The thing that builds the thing | You built a car in your garage. Now build a thousand. |
| 2 | A shop in a field | Your vibe coded app is a shop in a field you don't own. |
| 3 | Cool, it works. Now what? | Five questions no AI tool will ask you. |
| 4 | Knowing the name of a thing | Even if you know to ask for backups, do you know what good backups look like? |
| 5 | Value has moved | Infrastructure is being commoditised too. Judgement isn't. |
| 6 | Service your own car | AI doesn't close the gap between amateur and professional. It widens it. |
| 7 | Why build it at all | Yes, you could build your own CRM. Why would you? |
| 8 | Low stakes tool, high stakes data | A rota generator holds employee data. That's not a low stakes tool. |

Notes:
- Post 3 is the strongest. The five bold questions are already a list.
- Posts 1 and 2 are the most shareable. The analogies stand alone.
- Post 6 is the most likely to draw pushback, which is fine on LinkedIn.
- Each post needs its own closing line.
